Paste your game .zip files here (e.g. Granny_Forsaken.zip).

Rules:
- Keep them as .zip — do NOT unzip before uploading.
- Files over 25 MB must be uploaded with git/LFS (they are already LFS-tracked
  via .gitattributes), not the GitHub web UI.
- As soon as a zip lands here, the "1 - Port game (AssetRipper)" workflow runs
  automatically, or you can trigger it manually from the Actions tab.
